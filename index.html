Airport transfers

Request a wheelchair-accessible ride to or from the airport. During the pilot our team searches for options by hand and replies within 24 hours. Nothing is booked or charged until you accept an option.

  1. 1
    You send a requestWhere, when, and how you ride.
  2. 2
    We find a vehicleRamp or lift vans, or cars with room for your chair.
  3. 3
    You accept an optionWe book it and send you the driver's details.

TOTo the airport

Assistance desks usually ask you to arrive at least 2 hours before departure. We plan the pickup around that.

Leaving the aircraft with assistance can take 30 to 60 minutes after landing. We ask the driver to allow for it.

Where to find it

  • Weight and size are in the user manual or on the label under the seat. Measure in the travel position if it folds.
  • Battery Wh is printed on the battery label. If it only shows volts and Ah, use the calculator.
  • Handling notes are what you tell staff every time. Write them once here.

Your rights in brief

        
    
          
  • EU 1107/2006At EU airports, assistance for passengers with reduced mobility is free. Request it from the airline at least 48 hours ahead.
    • 
          
  • EU 1107/2006Up to two pieces of mobility equipment, wheelchair included, travel at no extra cost.
    • 
          
  • PIRIf your wheelchair arrives damaged, file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) at the baggage desk before leaving the airport.
    • 
          
  • 7 daysFor damage to checked items, send the airline a written claim within 7 days (Montreal Convention).
