How FreeVel works

Everything you need to fly with a wheelchair, in one place.

  • Handling cardOne card with your wheelchair's details and the right IATA codes, to show at check-in or print as PDF.
  • Which airlineSee which airlines can load your wheelchair upright, before you book.
  • Accessible transfersAsk for a vehicle with a ramp to and from the airport.
  • Damage claimIf something breaks, get a claim letter ready to send.

Create your profile in 4 steps

  1. Open My profileUse the button at the top right. No account is needed: your data stays on this device. Create a free account with email and password only if you want it on other devices too.
  2. Tell us about you and your wheelchairYour name, a phone number and the assistance you need. Then the wheelchair: type, make, weight and size measured in travel position, backrest folded.
  3. Add battery and handling notesFor power wheelchairs: battery type and watt-hours, written on its label, and how to switch it off. Add how staff should handle it. The progress bar shows what is still missing.
  4. Add your flightBack on My card, enter airline, flight number and date. Your card is ready to show at the desk or download as PDF.

Which airline fits your wheelchair?

We compare your wheelchair's travel size with the cargo door of the aircraft each airline flies from Italy. The lowest door decides whether it travels upright or gets tipped on its side.

Your wheelchair in travel position

Backrest folded, headrest, joystick and cushion removed. Height is what matters most.

These are the same measurements as in your profile: change them here or there.

How to read the result

  • Upright It goes through the hold door standing on its wheels. Lowest risk of damage.
  • Only on its side It fits only if staff tip it over. This is how frames, joysticks and wheels get damaged.
  • Does not fit No way through the door, even tipped.

We leave 3 cm of margin for handling. Airlines can change aircraft at short notice: always confirm with the airline when you book.

Where to find it

Your rights in brief

        
    
          
  • EU 1107/2006At EU airports, assistance for passengers with reduced mobility is free. Request it from the airline at least 48 hours ahead.
    • 
          
  • EU 1107/2006Up to two pieces of mobility equipment, wheelchair included, travel at no extra cost.
    • 
          
  • PIRIf your wheelchair arrives damaged, file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) at the baggage desk before leaving the airport.
    • 
          
  • 7 daysFor damage to checked items, send the airline a written claim within 7 days (Montreal Convention).
